aThe enzyme

Top2β is made of two identical copies (blue, lavender) that grip the DNA (gold) and cut both strands. DOX (coral) docks into the DNA at the cut site, deep inside the enzyme: Cut open removes the front half so you can see it.

bRigid docking

Docked as one rigid unit, the DOX–ICG pair lands away from the intercalation site: its DOX sits 17.6 Å from where DOX docked on its own binds (blue).

cNon-rigid docking
