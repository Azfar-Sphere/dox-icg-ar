aThe enzyme

Top2β is made of two identical copies (blue, lavender) that grip the DNA (gold) and cut both strands. DOX (coral) docks into the DNA at both cut sites, deep inside the enzyme: tap Cut open to remove the front half and see them.

bRigid docking

Docked as one rigid unit, the DOX–ICG pair lands away from the intercalation site at both cut sites: its DOX sits 17.6 and 17.7 Å from where DOX docked on its own binds (blue).

cNon-rigid docking

DOX docked alone fits the intercalation site; ICG then docks next to it at both cut sites (−10.9 and −10.3 kcal/mol) without a salt bridge (N–O 7.4 Å at both).
